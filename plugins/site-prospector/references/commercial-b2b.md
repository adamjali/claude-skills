# Commercial / B2B work for local trades

Added 2026-07-29. Everything else in this skill targets **B2C**: a homeowner searches, finds
the map pack, calls. Commercial does not work that way at all, and none of the B2C stack
transfers. This file is the separate method.

---

## 1) WHY THE B2C STACK DOES NOT TRANSFER

Local SEO, GBP, reviews, citations, referrals: all of it serves a homeowner who searches.

**A property manager does none of that.** They already have a vendor and switch on a failure
or a bid cycle. They ask other property managers, not Google. They run a bid with 3+ quotes.
They require a certificate of insurance, W-9 and workers comp before a real conversation.
They take months, sign contracts, and buy for a **portfolio**, not one address.

The universe is also tiny and **enumerable**. There may be 3,000 homeowners in a service area
who need lawn care this month. There are maybe **60 property management companies in the whole
county**, and you can list all of them.

> **B2C is a search problem. Commercial is a list problem.**

---

## 2) THE QUALIFYING GATE (ask before building anything)

**Can the client actually service a commercial account?** If not, generating commercial leads
actively harms them: they win one, fail it, and lose the reference permanently.

- **Capacity.** One HOA can exceed a solo operator's entire book. Can they absorb it without
  dropping residential customers?
- **Insurance.** Commercial buyers require a COI, typically $1M+ general liability, plus
  workers comp. Most one-truck operators carry neither.
- **Paperwork.** W-9, state license, sometimes a municipal business license.
- **Cash flow.** Commercial pays **net 30 to 60**. Residential pays same-day. This is what
  actually kills undercapitalized operators, and it is the least-discussed of the four.

**If any of the four fails, stop.** This is the capacity rule from the sales doctrine, sharper.

---

## 3) WHO ACTUALLY BUYS

Ordered by accessibility for a small trade:

| Buyer | Why |
|---|---|
| **HOA / condo management companies** | **The big one in my state.** One company controls dozens of communities. Land the manager, not the community |
| **Property management** (multifamily, rentals) | Recurring, portfolio-wide |
| **Realtors / investors** | Turnover cleanouts, listing prep, junk removal. Fast, repeat, low friction, easiest entry |
| **General contractors** | Sub work; they already need the trade |
| **Facility managers** | Retail, medical, dental, restaurants, storage, churches |
| **Municipal / school district** | Formal bids, slow, sticky for years |

My state ranks among the highest in the country for community-association density in the US, so **HOA and condo
management is the richest commercial channel available to a metro trade**, and it is worked
almost entirely by relationship rather than marketing.

---

## 4) THE CHANNELS THAT WORK

1. **A named list plus direct outreach.** ~80% of it. Every property/HOA management company in
   the service area, with a named contact, then call and email. **This is the existing scouting
   pipeline pointed at buyers instead of trades**, and the same VA economics apply
   (~$5 per 100 hand-built).
2. **Association membership.** CAI (Community Associations Institute) for HOA/condo; BOMA and
   IREM for commercial property; local apartment associations. They publish **member
   directories** and run events where the buyers physically are. Cheapest access to the room.
3. **Vendor prequalification.** Management companies keep approved-vendor lists. A form, a COI
   and a call puts you in the bid rotation permanently.
4. **Adjacent-trade referral.** The roofer knows the property manager; the GC subs out. Two
   trades swapping introductions beats any ad.
5. **LinkedIn.** Property and facility managers are there. Homeowners are not.
6. **Commercial-intent search.** Real but small: "commercial landscaping <county>", "HOA lawn
   maintenance contractor <city>". Low volume, very high value, so it is a **service page** (not
   the map pack) and it justifies Google Ads because a click is worth far more.

---

## 5) WHAT THE SITE NEEDS (build requirement)

Both benchmark sites reviewed 2026-07-28 already do this, which is the tell. Terra runs
"Commercial Landscaping Services" as its own service line; a nearby town's footer names the
buyers directly: *"your Property Management Group, Project Managers, Superintendents,
Contractors, HOA's."*

A commercial page needs:

- **Named buyer types in the copy**, so a property manager recognises themselves
- **Commercial proof, not homeowner proof:** insurance limits, workers comp, licensing, years,
  **number of crews**, response-time commitment, commercial references
- **Case studies of properties**, not driveways
- **A different CTA: "Request a bid" or "Get on our vendor list."** Never "free quote"
- **A downloadable capability sheet**, because they will forward it internally

---

## 6) WHAT WE CAN AND CANNOT DELIVER

**Can:** the commercial page and positioning, the named prospect list, outreach sequences and
templates, LinkedIn setup, commercial-intent SEO pages, Google Ads on commercial keywords, the
capability sheet.

**Cannot: the relationship.** Commercial is sold face to face over months. We can put the
client in front of the right 60 people. **The owner has to show up, follow up, and be there
when the incumbent fails.**

**Say this out loud when selling it.** It is the difference between a happy client and one who
expects the phone to ring and it does not. Same claims discipline as the standing rule against
ever quoting a lead-count number.

---

## 7) PRICING AND TIER PLACEMENT

**Not Foundation work.** It is a separate engagement: a setup project (page, list, materials,
association intros) plus a **monthly outbound retainer**, because it is outbound labour rather
than infrastructure. Realistically **Growth or Systems tier, or standalone.**

**Sell it on a different promise:** not "more leads," but *"you will be in front of the 60
people who control the contracts in your county."*

---

## 8) THE CHEAPEST FIRST MOVE

1. **Ask what percentage of revenue is commercial today, and whether they want more.** Some
   owners actively do not, because net-60 kills them.
2. **Build one list:** every property and HOA management company in the service area, named
   contact. VA job, same economics as any other list.
3. **Add the commercial page** with real proof and a bid-request CTA.
4. **Have them join the local CAI chapter.**

Steps 2 to 4 are about a week and cost almost nothing. **If the owner will not do the calling
implied by step 1's answer, stop.** Nothing downstream works without it.
