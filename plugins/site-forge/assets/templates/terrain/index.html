Protect What You Planted

Beds are the cheapest thing on a property to fix and the fastest to look neglected. A cut edge, the weeds out and fresh mulch at the right depth changes a front yard more than anything else for the money.

  • Cut Edge First: A defined bed line is what reads as maintained from the kerb. Mulch on an undefined bed still looks untidy.
  • Weeded Before, Not After: Mulching over live weeds buys a few weeks before they push straight back through.
  • Kept Off the Trunks: Mulch piled against a trunk holds damp on the bark. It gets pulled back rather than volcano-piled.
